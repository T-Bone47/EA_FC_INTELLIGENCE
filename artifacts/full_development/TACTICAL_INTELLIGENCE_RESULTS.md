# Phase 28 tactical intelligence evidence

- All defined formation slots are checked against their normalized model positions. The formation and slot must travel together. A slot assignment preserves its display role, including the CDM assignment mapped to CM in the existing 4-3-3 model.
- An isolated PostgreSQL-backed FC26 journey ran recommendation, comparison and what-if through V3. CDM and RCM assignments in the same formation produced different demands. Comparison scores matched the submitted recommendation context, and what-if carried the changed validated assignment.
- HTTP validation covers mismatched formation and position, contradictory tactical combinations, ambiguous V3 intent, unsupported constraints and explicit FC27 research opt-in.
- Account round-trip evidence covers typed stated default position and tactical style, and rejects unknown fields that pose as measured skill. The frontend submits those defaults only after the user chooses to apply them.
- Slot weights represent the application's tactical model. The approved role eligibility data and experimental evidence needed to evaluate game-mechanic effects remain open. The product has no observational skill model.
