# Squad intelligence results

Each stored formation slot maps to its actual V3.2 slot assignment. The squad analysis reports slot score, evidence coverage and weakest relative assignments where available. Suggestions exclude players already assigned to the squad. They show a positive gain when an eligible incumbent can be assessed.

The PostgreSQL test creates an owned FC27 squad, assigns a research player, confirms research opt-in and access controls, requests replacements for the same CDM slot, assigns the best returned player and verifies that the score matches the new analysis. A real browser repeated the core workflow.

This is a single-slot search under the selected tactic. Verified chemistry and market pricing remain unavailable, and full-squad multi-objective optimization remains open.
