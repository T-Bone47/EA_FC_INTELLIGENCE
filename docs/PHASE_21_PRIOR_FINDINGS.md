## What this session adds, not re-litigates

Per the mandate's own instruction ("Do not redo already-settled research unless new evidence requires it"), the sources above were not re-researched from scratch. Two pieces of genuinely new evidence did surface while preparing outreach materials (not by design, but because building a real outreach package required finding real contact information):

1. **FUT-DB.com's Terms Section 7** explicitly prohibits "reproduction, distribution, or modification" of their content without permission — a materially stronger finding than "silent on redistribution" recorded in Phase 20. This sharpens FUT-DB.com's classification and is reflected in `artifacts/phase21/provider_rights_matrix.json`.
2. **FUT-DB.com's legally-mandated German Impressum** (`FINAL FLASH GmbH`, `service@chilledcoins.com`) provides a real, actionable contact point that Phase 20 did not have. FutDB.app, by contrast, has **no located contact channel at all** — itself a finding worth recording, not an oversight in this phase's research.

No source moved to VERIFIED, GREEN, or an actionable YELLOW. This is reported plainly.
