## Why nothing was sent

The mandate is explicit: "Do not send anything automatically. Prepare the material for the user/provider contact." This system has no mechanism to send email or submit web forms on the project's behalf, and would not use one even if available without the project owner's direct action — outreach to a third party is a business decision with legal weight, not something to automate silently.

## What "prepared" means concretely

For FUT-DB.com and FUTBIN, a ready-to-send package exists: project description, exact fields needed, storage/derivation/display/retention terms, and the specific open legal question worth asking (respectively: does Section 7's redistribution clause cover derived-only output, and would a commercial licence be extended at all). A human operator can review, personalize, and dispatch these today.

For FutDB.app, preparation is blocked one step earlier — on finding out who to send it to. This is reported as a distinct, more basic gap, not conflated with "prepared but not yet sent."

## Immediate next action for a human operator

Send the FUT-DB.com and FUTBIN packages. Both have real, findable contact points and a narrow, answerable question. Locating a FutDB.app contact channel (e.g. by attempting the API key signup flow and seeing where a support email surfaces) is the prerequisite for that third package.
